Samples

Every card is a real program from the repository's samples/ folder: a tutorial to read in order, techniques that stand on their own, and generators modelled on real games. Each one runs live in your browser through WebAssembly: edit the source, change the seed or params, press Run, or step through the generation one rule application at a time (yellow outlines are matched cells, green are written).
